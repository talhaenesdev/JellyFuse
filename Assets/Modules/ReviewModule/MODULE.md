# Review

## Purpose
Asks Google Play to show its own rate-the-game sheet over the game. Game-agnostic: the game decides when, this module only asks.

## Concepts
review, rating, rate us, stars, in-app review, play store, IReviewService

## Decisions
- **Play decides whether the sheet appears.** It shows at most a few times a year per player and never says whether it did, so a caller asks and moves on - there is no answer to wait for.
- **Android only.** Everywhere else, including the Editor, asking logs a line and shows nothing.

## Known gaps
- The sheet only appears for a build installed from Play (internal or closed testing counts).
