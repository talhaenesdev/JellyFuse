# ConsentScreen

## Purpose
Asks the player once whether ads and analytics may use personal data (GDPR consent), and reports the answer. Accept or Refuse, no close button; ads run either way, unpersonalised on a refusal.

## Concepts
consent, GDPR, KVKK, privacy, personalised ads, advertising ID, ConsentScreenView

## Decisions
- **The panel only asks.** Whether to ask, and what follows from the answer, is MainModule's: `JudgeConsentCommand` at the end of the boot opens it when no answer is kept, and `ConsentAnswered` records it and starts LevelPlay and Unity Analytics with it.
- **Layer 7**, over the menu and any popup the boot opens beside it, under the loading overlay. It wears the settings panel art.

- **The answer can be changed.** The settings screen's privacy row opens the panel again; the new answer is recorded and passed on - LevelPlay hears it as it runs, and a withdrawal stops analytics.
