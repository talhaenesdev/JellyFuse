# Store

## Purpose
Real-money purchases through Unity IAP (Google Play Billing on Android). Lists the products its catalog names, announces their store prices, buys one on request and announces each product the store says was paid for. Game-agnostic: what a product gives belongs to the module that sells it.

## Concepts
store, IAP, in-app purchase, billing, google play billing, buy, price, product, restore, StoreSignals

## Decisions
- **A purchase is announced only once the server agrees.** On a device every order's purchase token goes to the `PurchaseVerify` Cloud Code module (`CloudCode/PurchaseVerify/` at the repo root), which asks the Google Play Developer API. Valid: `Purchased` is dispatched, the receiver grants and saves inside that dispatch, then the order is confirmed. Refused or unreachable: nothing is granted and the order stays unconfirmed - Play delivers it again next launch, and refunds one it never sold after three days.
- **A consumable already consumed is refused.** That is the replay guard: the same token shown twice is granted once.
- **The store never signs in.** It waits up to 20 seconds for the account module's sign-in before calling the server. The Editor's fake store is not verified.
- **Owned non-consumables are announced again at every launch.** That is the restore: a reinstalled game gets its no-ads back without a button. A receiver's grant for one has to be idempotent.
- **Prices come from the store, never from the asset.** Until the store answers the shop shows nothing to buy with; the price string is the store's localized one.

## Known gaps
- Every product id in `CD_StoreCatalog` has to exist in Play Console (Monetize > Products > In-app products), active, and the build has to be uploaded to a testing track before Play will sell anything.
- The server needs a Google service account with access to the app in Play Console, its JSON key in the Unity project's Secret Manager as `GOOGLE_PLAY_SERVICE_ACCOUNT`, and the module deployed (Deployment window, `Assets/CloudCode/PurchaseVerify.ccmr`). Until then every device purchase stays pending and nothing is granted.
- A non-consumable token is not tied to one player: a shared no-ads token restores on any account.
