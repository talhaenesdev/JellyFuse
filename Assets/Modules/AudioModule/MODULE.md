# Audio

## Purpose
Reusable sound service: plays short sound effects by cue and loops music by track, from clips listed in CD_Audio, and keeps the player's sounds and music preferences. Knows no gameplay modules.

## Concepts
audio, sound, sfx, music, volume, mute, AudioSource, IAudioService, CD_Audio, AudioCue, MusicTrack

## Integration
- A Service with no public signals: callers inject IAudioService (Play, PlayMusic, SetSoundsEnabled,
  SetMusicEnabled), or bind its steps, `IAudioService.Commands.Play` and `.PlayMusic`, in a sequence.
- AudioServiceRoot is a prefab (Prefabs/), Initialize Order -34, next to HapticServiceRoot in MainScene.
  CD_Audio is filed in its RootAdapter's own Scriptables slot; AudioModel reads it, Commands never do.
- CD_Audio maps each AudioCue to clips (one picked at random), volume, pitch jitter and a repeat
  interval, and each MusicTrack to a looping clip. Music crossfades over MusicCrossfade seconds.
- Preferences are PlayerPrefs `Audio.Sounds` and `Audio.Music`; the Settings and Pause screens toggle
  them through the service. Music switched back on resumes the track asked for last.
- UiClickSounds: every frame a ClickTick runs DetectUiClickCommand, which plays Tap when a press is
  released over an interactable Button or Toggle. No screen wires anything for it.
- Gameplay: PlayPuzzleSoundCommand (grab, merge, deliver, win, lose) follows the haptic feedback step;
  boosters, undo, coins and time-out bind their cue in GameplaySystemContext. The menu screen plays
  MusicTrack.Menu, a level start MusicTrack.Gameplay.

## Sounds and licence
Every clip under Art/Audio was synthesised in the Unity Editor for this project, from code: no sample,
library or third-party recording is in them, so they need no licence or attribution. Effects are
ADPCM, decompressed on load; music is Vorbis, streamed.

## Known gaps
- No test module yet; checked in MainScene Play Mode only.
- The clips are simple synthesis. Replacing one is a drop-in: point CD_Audio at the new clip.
