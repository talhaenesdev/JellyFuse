# GameplayScreenModule

## Purpose
The playable Jelly Fuse board and HUD, opened when the player presses Play. Shows the walls,
the doors and the jelly each waits for, animated jellies, timer, move count, invalid-shape warnings, undo, restart and a solution hint.

## Concepts
gameplay screen, run, HUD

## Presentation
GameplayScreen owns the HUD and the board. The board is JellySpriteBoardView: UI sprites in a
canvas of its own, drawn from the model's snapshots. It holds no placement, fusion or exit rule;
it only shows what the snapshot says (FuseReady included) and turns pointer positions into cells.

- Scriptables/CD_JellyPresentation.asset links the screen's settings, one asset per concern:
  CD_JellyFeel (motion, fuse hint, delivery), CD_JellyBoardLook (art, shading, colours, palette,
  wall skin) and CD_GameplayHud (HUD, map placement, panels). Each previews in Play Mode; the
  board look edits the palette in place.
- Scriptables/CD_JellyWallSkin.asset: the wall and door art, one slot per straight side, corner,
  inner-wall join and door width. JellySpriteBoardView.Walls picks the piece by position.
- Sprites/Walls: the wall sprites (SPR_Wall_*), copied from Assets/Art and imported as sprites.
- Art comes from Assets/Art/Textures/Gameplay, packed by Atlas_Gameplay.
- Jellies slide along the route the model reports, lift smoothly, and come apart on undo.
- PRESENTATION.md describes every setting.
- The HUD and the result panels are authored in Prefabs/GameplayScreen (Objects are named Type_What: Btn_ a button, Txt_ a text, Img_ an image, Pnl_ a container or card.): Pnl_Coins, Pnl_Level,
  Btn_Undo, Pnl_Clock, Btn_Settings, Pnl_Board (the board is drawn in code inside it), Txt_Warning,
  Btn_Restart, Pnl_WinDim/Pnl_WinCard, Pnl_LoseDim/Pnl_LoseCard. Colours, sizes and positions live
  there; CD_GameplayHud keeps only map placement, the timer warning and the panel delays.
- Texts are keyed (gameplay.*) and read through LocalizationModule; the English in GameplayScreenView
  is the fallback. OpenGameplayScreenCommand hands the screen ILocalizationService.Get, and
  ApplyLanguage (wired from LanguageChanged in LocalizationConnectorSubContext) redraws them.
  Translations: GameplayModule/Scriptables/Localization/CD_GameplayTexts.
